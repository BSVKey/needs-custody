How the pilot works

  1. Try it for $5, or pay $250 once. Card, or Bitcoin BSV credits. The $5 test pilot runs 3 days with up to 100 receipts. The full pilot covers one site for 90 days, up to 10,000 receipts and a daily public seal. Nothing renews, and a test pilot upgrades for $245.
  2. Invite your people. Make an invite for each role (driver, water tester, camp committee, kiosk, warehouse, carrier, distribution point, rain gauge). Each invite is a QR code that works once.
  3. They scan and record. The phone makes its own signing key, so nobody else can sign for them. Records work with no signal and send when a connection returns.
  4. Share the live page. Donors and auditors see trucks, food, kiosk sales and rain checked in their own browser, with every day sealed in the public record.
  5. Keep everything. Download all records at any time. After 90 days the page stays up and the records stay checkable.
