Pilot guide · about 10 minutes to set up

Run a pilot yourself, step by step.

Everything you need to go from nothing to a live, public record of water, food and aid at your site. Start with a $5 test pilot. You can upgrade it later without starting over.

Watch it first (2 minutes)

With narration. The video was recorded on a test copy, so its public seal is simulated. On needs.bsvkey.com every seal is a real Bitcoin BSV transaction.

Before you start

  • A computer or phone for you, the pilot owner, to set things up.
  • One phone per person who records. Any recent smartphone with a browser works. No app store and no SIM needed, only a connection now and then.
  • $5 for a test pilot, or $250 for the full 90 days. Pay by card, or with credits you add in Bitcoin BSV.
  • Your household list, if you'll track food: ration card numbers and how many kg each household gets.

Step by step

1

Create your account

Go to needs.bsvkey.com/pilot. Under New here, enter your organization and an email for receipts, then press Create my account.

2

Save your key

You'll see a key that starts with ck_. It's the only way back into your pilot, and it's shown once. Press Download key file or Copy, keep it somewhere safe, then press I've saved it.

Next time, sign in under I have a key with the same key.

3

Add your site and pick a plan

Press Add a pilot site. Choose:

  • Test pilot, $5: 3 days, up to 100 receipts, sealed for real.
  • Full pilot, $250: 90 days, up to 10,000 receipts.

Give the site a name and the number of people it serves. The drought settings can stay as they are unless you have your own trigger.

4

Pay

By card: press Pay by card and finish on the secure card page. You come back to a running pilot.

In Bitcoin BSV: open the credits page, sign in with the same key, add credits in BSV, then come back and press Pay from balance.

5

Your pilot is running

The site shows how many days are left, receipts used, people and sealed days. On a test pilot, Upgrade turns it into the full 90-day pilot for $245: the $5 you paid counts toward the $250 for 30 days, and you keep the site, its people and its receipts.

6

Invite your people

Under Invite someone, pick a role, type the name to show on the card, and press Make invite. Print the card or copy the link and send it privately. Each invite works once, on one phone. Make one invite per person.

The live page is public, so use a first name or a role on the card ("Driver, truck 7"), not a full name.

7

Each person joins on their phone

They scan the QR code with their phone's camera. The phone makes its own signing key, which never leaves the phone, so nobody else can sign records as them. They press Start recording, and it's best to add the page to the home screen.

Lost or replaced phone? Remove the person on your console and make a new invite. Records already sent stay.

8

Record, even with no signal

Each phone shows only the form for its role. The driver enters liters and picks the water test for that load, then presses Sign and save.

With no signal the dot turns red and the record waits on the phone. When a connection returns it sends by itself, and the dot turns green.

9

The receiver signs for what arrived

The camp committee picks the delivery and enters the liters that actually arrived. If the truck billed 10,000 L and 7,200 L arrived, the gap is now on the record, signed by both sides.

10

Share your live page

On your console, press Open the live page or Copy link. Send it to donors, auditors or your board. They see every truck, kiosk sale, food consignment and rain reading, checked in their own browser. Short deliveries, unsafe water, missing food and double claims are flagged automatically.

11

Each day is sealed

Once a day is over (midnight UTC), its receipts are sealed together in the public record on Bitcoin BSV the next time the live page is opened, or when you press Seal finished days now. After that, nobody can change them, including us. The Public seal card turns green once your browser has checked it. You can download all records at any time.

Questions

  • Is the $5 test real? Yes. It's a real payment, real receipts and real daily seals, just short (3 days) and small (100 receipts). If you upgrade within 30 days, the $5 counts toward the $250.
  • What does it cost after 90 days? Nothing renews automatically. The live page and records stay up and checkable. Start a new pilot when you're ready.
  • What if there's no internet for days? Phones keep everything and send when they can, and each record keeps the time it was made. Make sure phones connect at least once before the pilot ends: records sent after the end date aren't accepted.
  • Can someone fake a record? Only the phone a person joined with can sign as them. An edited, missing or out-of-order record is flagged on the live page. A receipt shows who said something, not that it's true: two people working together could still sign for water that never came, but the record carries both their names.
  • Are households identified? No. Ration card numbers become private codes on the warehouse and distribution phones. The live page never shows a name or a card number.
  • Who can see the live page? Anyone with the link. Use first names or roles on invite cards.
  • Refunds? Payments buy credits for BSVKey services, which are not refundable for cash. Questions: embryospace@gmail.com.
